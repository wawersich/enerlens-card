Extra values at the consumers

Like the nodes, every consumer in the list can show more than its power: a ring around its icon, a thin bar at the top or bottom of the row, an icon that fills. It can also be pinned to the top or bottom, kept in the list always or under a condition, and highlighted.

Watch: one round takes a minute and a half. The wallbox is pinned to the top and highlighted; while it does not charge it shows the car’s state of charge. The heat pump runs and rests - at rest the hot-water temperature stands in place of the power, its bars show hot water and flow. The buffer tank fills, the 3D printer shows its progress, the washing machine stands at the bottom.

Hover an icon or a bar with the mouse: its value shows with its label, such as "Hot water 52 °C" - in this demo too. A tap on the row opens Home Assistant’s window with the history of the power and of every extra value.

This is the YAML:

consumers:
  - entity: sensor.wallbox_power
    name: Wallbox
    position: top                            # pinned to the top
    show: always                             # always in the list
    highlight: { bold: true, band: true }
    indicators:
      ring: { entity: sensor.car_battery_level }
      idle_value: ring                       # without power: the charge
  - entity: sensor.heat_pump_power
    name: Heat pump
    min_w: 40
    show: always
    indicators:
      bar_top: { entity: sensor.hot_water, min: 40, max: 60, label: Hot water }
      bar_bottom:
        entity: sensor.flow_temperature
        min: 20
        max: 55
        label: Flow
        color: "#e53935"                     # a colour of its own
      idle_value: bar_top                    # below 40 W: "52 °C"
  - entity: sensor.heating_rod_power
    name: Buffer tank
    icon: mdi:water-boiler
    indicators:
      fill: { entity: sensor.buffer_charge, label: Charge }
  - entity: sensor.printer_power
    name: 3D printer
    indicators:
      bar_top: { entity: sensor.print_progress, label: Print progress }
  - entity: sensor.washing_machine_power
    position: bottom
    highlight: { band: true }

Indicators

The ring is one of four indicators every consumer can have, one at a time or together, with no extra room in the row:

Place Look

ring

ring around the icon or colour dot

bar_top, bar_bottom

thin bar at the top or bottom of the row, from the name to the value

fill

the colour dot fills from below; with an icon, a field behind it does

Each place has an entity, a range min to max (0 to 100 by default) and optionally a label; ring and bars have a colour of their own (in the editor under Colours), the fill takes the consumer’s. One example is the hot-water temperature of a heat pump:

consumers:
  - entity: sensor.heat_pump_power
    name: Heat pump
    min_w: 40
    show: always
    indicators:
      bar_top:
        entity: sensor.hot_water_temperature
        min: 40
        max: 60
        label: Hot water
      idle_value: bar_top                    # below 40 W: "52 °C" instead of the power

Value column (idle_value): with power it shows the power. Below the row’s minimum power (min_w, else min_consumer_w) it shows the chosen value, with its unit, as Home Assistant shows it. The column switches on the list’s beat, not with every reading.

Tooltip and tap: hover the icon or a bar for the value with its label; a tap on the row opens the window with the power and below it every value in a chart of its own.

Energy mode: the indicators are left out there, like the extra values at the nodes. With in_energy: true they stay and show the value of now; the tooltip then says "· now", the value column keeps showing kWh.

Older spellings: charge (with soc and label) means a ring from 0 to 100 % with idle_value: ring and in_energy: true; charge.show with plugged and unplugged means show and condition. Both still work. Changing the consumer in the editor writes it in the new form.

Pin, keep, highlight

This works for every consumer, not only the wallbox.

Position (position): top or bottom fixes the row at the top or bottom of the list; several stand in the order of the configuration, sorted by dragging in the editor. Without it the row sorts by power. The position only decides the place, not whether the row appears.

Show in the list (show): from its minimum power a consumer is listed anyway. always keeps it there at all times as well, condition only while the condition is met - at 0 W too. No setting hides a consumer that draws power.

Condition (condition): a sensor, an operator and a value. "=" and "!=" take one or more states ("is one of them" / "is none of them"), ">", "<", ">=" and "⇐" exactly one number, e.g. sensor.battery_voltage ">" 10.5. The operators are quoted in YAML. While the sensor is briefly unavailable, its last answer counts. For more - a range, an attribute, two conditions - build a helper in Home Assistant and use it as the sensor.

Maximum (max_consumers): held rows count and get their places first; the strongest consumers fill the rest. If more rows are held than the maximum allows, all held rows still appear - the editor then says that no other consumer will.

Highlight (highlight): bold: true sets name and value in bold, band: true lightly backs the row in the consumer’s colour; either on its own or both, and no extra room.

In the energy mode position and always apply the same way; a condition describes the state now and is not evaluated there.

The rest row

The rest is the house value minus the consumers shown. It can be set like a consumer - with an entry type: rest instead of an entity in the consumer list:

consumers:
  - entity: sensor.wallbox_power
    position: top
  - type: rest
    name: Other
    icon: mdi:cloud-question
    position: bottom
    show: always
    highlight: { band: true }
    indicators:
      bar_bottom: { entity: sensor.outside_temperature, min: -10, max: 35, label: Outside }
  - entity: sensor.washing_machine_power
    position: bottom
  • Name, icon, colour (name, icon, color) as for a consumer.
  • Minimum power (min_w): below it the rest disappears; without it min_consumer_w applies. The filter button does not lift it.
  • Position (position): top or bottom. Among the pinned rows the place of the entry in the list counts - in the example the rest stands at the bottom, above the washing machine. Without a position it is sorted in by power.
  • Show in the list (show: always): the rest stays below its minimum power too. Negative, or without a house value, it shows "—" instead of a figure. It does not count towards the maximum number of consumers.
  • Highlight and extra values as for a consumer - the extra values with any entity, e.g. the outside temperature.

Not for the rest: a condition and "Show without power"; it has no entity and no device threshold of its own.

In the editor the rest is an entry of its own in the consumer list, without a remove button, moved by dragging. Its colour is under Colours.

Older spelling: list.rest_label, icons.rest and colors.rest still apply. Change the rest in the editor and it writes the entry type: rest, taking these values along.
