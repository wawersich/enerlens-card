= Animations
:page-key: animation
:nav-order: 10.5

The card moves two things: the consumer ring when the order of the consumers
changes, and the dots on the lines that show the flow. Both can be set. Under
_Flows_ → *Motion*, `off` stops every movement; the default `auto` follows the
device's "reduce motion" setting.

== The ring

The list sorts itself by power on a fixed beat, and the ring follows it. There
are three ways it can do that:

* *Overtake* (default): every segment travels to its new place. One that moves
forward dives to an inner lane, passes underneath the others and surfaces at
its place.
* *Cross-fade colours*: the places stay where they are, their colours blend
into the new order. Calmer, above all when the order changes often.
* *Jump*: no movement, the ring stands in the new order at once.

Try it: in this card four consumers trade places every four seconds, one
segment passing one, two or all three of the others. List and quick buttons are hidden, so that only the
ring is to be seen.

== The dots

The dots show where power flows. Their speed and number depend on the power:
below the *minimum power* (default 10 W) none runs, above it one slow dot
first; with more power it gets faster, then more join, until top speed and the
*maximum dots* are reached. Where that is, one value says: the *peak power of
the plant* (default 6000 W); for more control the single thresholds are under
*Fine-tune the motion*.

What a dot looks like is its *design*: *Plain dots* (default) are a circle with
nothing else, the others give it a bright core and, depending on the design, a
glow. The design changes only the look, never speed or number.

Try it: here all values stand still and nothing re-sorts - only the dots move.
The switches choose the design, the sliders the maximum number of dots (the
card takes 2 to 10, default 5), the dot size (40 to 150 %, default 100) and
the speed: seconds a run at the lower and
at the upper threshold, default 5 and 1.8.

[source,yaml]
----
ring:
  animation: fade          # overtake | fade | jump
flow:
  animation: auto          # auto | on | off
  design: neutrino
  max_dots: 8
  slow_s: 8                # seconds a run with little power
  fast_s: 3                # ... with a lot of power
----
