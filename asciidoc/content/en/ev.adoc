= Wallbox and EV
:page-key: ev
:nav-order: 6

A wallbox is a consumer like any other. The car's state of charge comes from
the car's own integration, not from the wallbox - Home Assistant does not link
the two, so the consumer is told which one belongs to it.

Try it: the wallbox charges for a minute and the ring around its icon fills
up; then it pauses for half a minute, and the row shows the charge instead of
the power. In Home Assistant a tap on the row shows power and charge over time.

++++
<div class="card-demo" data-demo="ev" data-yaml="off"></div>
++++

This is the YAML:

[source,yaml]
----
consumers:
  - entity: sensor.wallbox_power
    name: Wallbox
    icon: mdi:ev-station
    charge:
      soc: sensor.car_battery_level          # the car, in %
      show: plugged                          # charging (default) | plugged | always
      plugged: binary_sensor.car_charge_cable
----

In the editor the consumer unfolds; under *State of charge* are the *State of
charge entity (%)*, when it shows - *While charging*, *While plugged in* or
*Always* - and, for *While plugged in*, the cable:

A thin ring around the icon shows the charge. While the car charges, the row
shows the power; otherwise it shows the charge instead, so the row needs no
extra room. With `show: plugged` the row stays in the list as long as the car
is plugged in - full and at 0 W too. `plugged` can also be a wallbox status
sensor; `unplugged: ++[++available, none++]++` then lists the states that mean "not
plugged in". When the car is asleep and its sensor unavailable, the last known
charge is shown grey. Hover the icon for the value; a tap on the row opens a
window with the power and the charge below it (see
link:energy.html#detail[Energy]); in
the editor it is under the consumer, _State of charge_. It is not limited to
cars - a power station works the same way, and so does a 3D printer with its
print progress. "State of charge" does not fit there: under _Label_ (in YAML
`charge.label`) put e.g. "Print progress" instead - in the tooltip and in the
detail window.
