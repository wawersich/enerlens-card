= Arrangements
:page-key: layout
:nav-order: 5.5

Where solar, grid, house and battery stand is up to you: the cross, two
squares turned by 45° and a triangle. What the card shows is the same in
every arrangement - figures, rings, dots, lists. The card on this page runs
on invented values with three PV sources.

Try it: the switches above the card change what you would change in the
editor; below it is what of that ends up in YAML.

++++
<div class="card-demo" data-demo="arrange" data-controls="layout,lines,pvrows,list"></div>
++++

== Cross

Solar at the top, grid on the left, house on the right, battery at the
bottom - the arrangement the card started with, and the default. The
connections are curved.

== Square · solar right

Turned by 45°: solar top right, house bottom right, grid top left, battery
bottom left. Both lists stand on the right, the PV rows level with solar,
the consumers level with the house.

== Square · solar left

Turned the other way: solar top left, house top right, grid bottom left,
battery bottom right. The PV rows stand left of the drawing, the consumers
right of it - each fan runs to its own node. That needs about 590 px of
width; on a narrower card, a phone or a column of the sections view, both
lists stand below.

== Triangle

Solar, house and battery one above the other, the grid left of the house.
The triangle is taller than wide and so never wider than 320 px. Without a
battery its lowest third goes.

== Setting it

In the editor, *Display* has *Arrangement* and *Line form* at the top. The
line form is curved for the cross and straight for the other arrangements;
both can be changed.

[.only-light]
image::editor-layout-en-light.png["Arrangement and line form in the Display section",490]

[.only-dark]
image::editor-layout-en-dark.png["Arrangement and line form in the Display section",490]

In the energy mode the arrows at grid and battery point towards the middle of
the drawing in every arrangement when energy was imported or discharged, and
away from it for export and charging.

[source,yaml]
----
layout: square_left        # cross | square_right | square_left | triangle
flow:
  lines: curved            # curved | straight
----
